<h3>Given (in the grey header)</h3>
<p><code>n</code>: an integer (1 &le; n &le; 100&nbsp;000)</p>

<h3>Task</h3>
<p><code>print()</code> the sum of all integers from 1 to <code>n</code> (inclusive) that are divisible by 3 <strong>or</strong> by 5 (or both).</p>

<h3>Sample</h3>
<div class="samples">
  <div>
    <strong>Header</strong>
    <pre>n = 10</pre>
  </div>
  <div>
    <strong>Output</strong>
    <pre>33</pre>
  </div>
</div>
<p class="muted small">3 + 5 + 6 + 9 + 10 = 33.<br>Numbers divisible by both, such as 15, must only be counted once.</p>
