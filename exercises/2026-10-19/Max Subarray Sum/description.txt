<p>You are given <code>N</code> days of profit and loss figures in the list <code>a</code>. Find the <strong>largest possible sum</strong> of a non-empty run of consecutive days.</p>

<h3>Given (in the grey header)</h3>
<p><code>n</code>: the number of days (1 &le; n &le; 100&nbsp;000)<br>
<code>a</code>: a list of <code>n</code> integers, each between &minus;1000 and 1000</p>

<h3>Task</h3>
<p><code>print()</code> a single integer: the maximum sum of any non-empty contiguous block of <code>a</code>.</p>

<h3>Sample</h3>
<div class="samples">
  <div>
    <strong>Header</strong>
    <pre>n = 8
a = [-2, 1, -3, 4, -1, 2, 1, -5]</pre>
  </div>
  <div>
    <strong>Output</strong>
    <pre>6</pre>
  </div>
</div>
<p class="muted small">The best block is <code>4 -1 2 1</code>.</p>

<h3>Notes</h3>
<p>Large cases are included, so an O(N&sup2;) approach will time out. Aim for a single pass.</p>
